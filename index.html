Launch site • Latvia • 1991
POGA & EKO — PART 1: THE BEGINNING Latvia, 1991. The launch site was packed. People had gathered to watch the biggest space mission the country had ever attempted. At the centre of the platform stood two astronauts: Poga, a gray cat, and Eko, a corgi. Their shuttle waited behind them, engines ready and mission lights flashing. MISSION CONTROL: "Astronauts Poga and Eko, final checks complete. You are cleared for launch." Eko adjusted his helmet. Poga checked the control tablet one last time. EKO: "So... space?" POGA: "That's generally where rockets go." The crowd counted down. TEN. NINE. EIGHT... The engines ignited. Flames rolled beneath the shuttle as it lifted from the platform. The crowd watched the vehicle climb through the clouds. Inside, Eko gripped his seat while Poga calmly monitored the instruments. EKO: "We're actually doing it." POGA: "Try not to touch anything." Earth grew smaller in the window. Latvia disappeared beneath the clouds, and the two astronauts entered orbit. MISSION CONTROL: "Poga, Eko, welcome to space." Their mission had officially begun.
